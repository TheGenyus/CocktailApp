Nom: Apple & Elderflower Collins

Image: https://cdn.diffordsguide.com/cocktail/LRZ0Lr/default/0/512x.webp?v=1737701571

Ingrédients:
- 45 ml
- Hayman's London Dry Gin
- 30 ml
- Elderflower liqueur
- 30 ml
- Apple Schnapps liqueur
- 30 ml
- Lime juice (freshly squeezed)
- 30 ml
- Thomas Henry Soda Water
- chilled
Recette / Préparation:
SHAKE first 4 ingredients with ice and strain into ice-filled glass. TOP with soda and briefly stir.
