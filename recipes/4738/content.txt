Ingrédients:
- 45 ml
- Havana Club Añejo 7 Años Cuban Rum
- 90 ml
- *Special ingredient #1 (see above)
- 15 ml
- Vanilla sugar syrup
- 15 ml
- Exprè Caffè Italiano (or freshly made hot Espresso Coffee)
- 5 gram
- Freshly grated nutmeg
- 22.5 ml

Recette / Préparation:
