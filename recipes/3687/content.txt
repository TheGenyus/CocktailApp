- 45 ml
- Cognac (brandy)
- 15 ml
- Mastiha liqueur
- 5 ml
- Fernet Branca liqueur

- Sugar syrup 'rich' (2 sugar to 1 water, 65.0°Brix)
- 1 dash
- Bob's Chocolate bitters
