ID: 4409
Nom: Back in Black
Source: https://www.diffordsguide.com/cocktails/recipe/4409/back-in-black
Image: https://cdn.diffordsguide.com/cocktail/RJ2qwR/default/0/512x.webp?v=1737701599

Ingrédients:
- 45 ml
- Light white rum (charcoal-filtered 1-4 years old)
- 15 ml
- Amaro (e.g. Averna)
- 15 ml
- Sugar syrup 'simple' (1 sugar to 1 water, 48°Brix)
- 10 ml
- Lime juice (freshly squeezed)
- 1 dash
- Angostura Aromatic Bitters
- Top up with
- Thomas Henry Tonic Water
Recette / Préparation:
VERSEZ tous les ingrédients dans un verre rempli de glace et remuez.

Profil (force/goût):
Score force (0-10): None
Score douceur/acidité (0-10): None
Note expert (1-5): 3.5
Note membres (1-5): 3.5

Avis:
