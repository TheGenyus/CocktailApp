Ingrédients:
- 60 ml
- Ketel One Citroen Vodka
- 15 ml
- Giffard Vanille de Madagascar
- 45 ml
- Pineapple juice
- 15 ml
- Lime juice (freshly squeezed)
- 7.5 ml
- Lime cordial (sweetened lime juice)
Recette / Préparation:
